1. Regression fit, common sample (AIC/BIC comparable)

Common sample: 3,055 days from GDXJ's inception (2009-11-12) to 2021-12-31. Best BIC: GLD+TLT+GDXJ (R² 0.906). Every model that includes GDXJ explains far more of GDX (R² ≈ 0.90–0.91) than any without it (≤ 0.60): GDXJ is itself a basket of gold miners, so with it in the model the residual becomes GDX-versus-junior-miners relative value rather than GDX-versus-gold. IAU and GLD together give a maximum VIF of 324: near-perfectly collinear, the split between their two betas is noise even though the fit barely changes.
